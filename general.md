# LiveSense AI: General Overview

## 1. Introduction
**LiveSense AI** is a next-generation, IoT-driven precision dairy farming platform. It is designed to monitor the health and well-being of dairy cows in real-time using smart wearable collars. By leveraging Artificial Intelligence (AI) directly on the collar (Edge Computing), the system provides early detection of diseases—specifically **Mastitis**—before severe clinical symptoms appear.

## 2. The Problem It Solves
Dairy farms often struggle with delayed disease detection. When a cow gets mastitis (an udder infection):
- Milk production drops significantly.
- Veterinary costs increase.
- Animal welfare is compromised.
- Contaminated milk can lead to discarded batches.

Traditional monitoring relies on visual inspection or late-stage indicators (like milk quality drops). LiveSense AI shifts this to **proactive, continuous monitoring** based on behavioral changes.

## 3. How It Works (The User Journey)

### Step 1: Farm Onboarding
A farm owner registers an account on the LiveSense AI platform. Each farm gets a secure, isolated workspace.

### Step 2: Equipping the Herd
The farmer attaches a smart collar to a cow and registers the cow in the digital dashboard (e.g., "Cow 2001"). 

### Step 3: Continuous Monitoring
The collar continuously tracks the cow's micro-movements, grazing patterns, and resting times. Instead of sending all this raw, heavy data over the internet, the collar acts as a "mini-computer." It uses AI to analyze the data right on the cow's neck. 

### Step 4: Real-Time Alerts
The collar occasionally "phones home" to the cloud, sending a lightweight status update (e.g., "Cow 2001 is HEALTHY" or "Cow 2001 is SUSPECTED SICK"). 
If a cow is flagged as sick:
- An immediate alert pops up on the farmer's dashboard.
- A **Medical Ticket** is automatically generated.
- The farmer can isolate the cow and consult a veterinarian immediately.

## 4. Key Features & Benefits
- **Multi-Tenant Dashboard:** Every farm has its own secure login and manages its own herd independently.
- **Real-Time Data:** The dashboard updates instantly without needing to refresh the page when a cow gets sick.
- **Early Intervention:** Catching mastitis early reduces antibiotic use and saves milk yield.
- **Cost Efficiency:** Because the AI runs on the collar, farms don't need expensive high-speed internet to transmit gigabytes of raw sensor data; they only transmit tiny text alerts.

## 5. Summary
In short, LiveSense AI turns standard dairy farms into "smart farms." It acts as a 24/7 digital veterinarian for every single cow, ensuring maximum herd health, higher profitability, and better animal welfare.
